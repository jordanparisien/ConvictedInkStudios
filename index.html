About the Artist
The Artist

Jordan Parisien

Jordan Parisien has been tattooing for over eight years out of Turtle Mountain. He works across black and grey realism, illustrative, fine line, lettering, floral, traditional, and color, but the work he's known for is Ojibwe floral, portraits, and memorial pieces. Every piece starts with a conversation: what it means, where it goes, and how it'll hold up for the rest of your life.

Deposits & Booking

Consultations are free.

A $100 non-refundable deposit locks in each session day. Multi-day pieces need a $100 deposit for each day.

Your deposit comes off that session's total.

Deposits are not refunded for client cancellations.

Need to reschedule? Give at least 48 hours' notice and your deposit moves to the new date.

Cancel with less than 48 hours' notice or don't show, and the deposit covers the time that was held for you. That's it. You won't be charged beyond the deposit.

If I have to cancel, your deposit moves to a new date or gets refunded, your choice.
